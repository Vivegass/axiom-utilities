# Changelog

## 1.0.0+mc26.3

First public release, for Minecraft 26.3 and Axiom 6.1.x.

- **Offset target point** for every Axiom tool, under *Placement* in Tool Options (per tool).
- **Paint in air next to blocks** for Painter, Noise Painter and Gradient Painter, with *Layer thickness* 1–6.
- **Global presets**: save and load brush settings, placement, tool mask and active block; pick what to save and what to load; list or block-tile view; loads on click.
- **Block pipette** in the Select Block dialog: click a block in the world to pick it, `Esc` cancels.
- Settings from the pre-release builds ("Axiom Brush Offset") are moved to `config/axiom-utilities/` automatically.
